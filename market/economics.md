# MAD Bounty fee economics (1% on accepted GMV), assumptions labeled

- Lean (domain+infra, no audit, no salary): annual cost $300 -> break-even accepted GMV $30,000/yr ($2,500/mo)
- Audit amortized 2y ($30K) + infra $2K/yr: annual cost $17,000 -> break-even accepted GMV $1,700,000/yr ($141,667/mo)
- Above + 0.25 FTE maintainer ($40K/yr): annual cost $57,000 -> break-even accepted GMV $5,700,000/yr ($475,000/mo)
- Above + 1 FTE ($150K/yr all-in): annual cost $167,000 -> break-even accepted GMV $16,700,000/yr ($1,391,667/mo)

GMV needed at avg bounty size (for the $57K scenario):
- avg $5: 1,140,000 accepted bounties/yr (21,923/week)
- avg $20: 285,000 accepted bounties/yr (5,481/week)
- avg $50: 114,000 accepted bounties/yr (2,192/week)
- avg $200: 28,500 accepted bounties/yr (548/week)
