# MAD Bounty: tactics we will not use

These are dishonest, break platform rules, or get filtered out. Each one also carries a practical cost. For a protocol that asks agents and people to trust it with money, being caught astroturfing does more damage than having no marketing at all. Sources were accessed 2026-11-25.

| Tactic | Why not | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Mass AI-generated articles or near-duplicate pages** (one post rewritten 30 ways, "programmatic SEO" pages per keyword) | Google calls this *scaled content abuse*: "using generative AI tools… to generate many pages without adding value", including "creating multiple sites with the intent of hiding the scaled nature". Pretraining pipelines (FineWeb, DCLM, Nemotron-CC) dedupe and quality-filter, so the copies get dropped anyway. | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#scaled-content; https://arxiv.org/html/2406.17557 |
| **Content farms / guest-post networks / paid "dofollow" listings** (including mcp.so's $39 "dofollow project link" tier) | Google's link-spam policy covers buying links that pass ranking credit. It devalues our domain and the directory's. Free listings are fine. | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies; https://mcp.so/submit |
| **Keyword stuffing** in registry descriptions, SKILL.md or llms.txt (e.g. "bounty escrow AI agent USDC Base x402 MCP best #1") | It's in Google's spam list ("many pages… contains search keywords"). Registries rank by quality; the x402 Bazaar has a metadata content policy, and Glama grades quality. Agents choose tools from their descriptions, so a stuffed description makes tool selection worse. | Google spam policies; https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/seller/get-discovered (metadata content policy) |
| **Fake GitHub stars, follows, forks, or star-for-star rings** | GitHub AUP §4 bans "inauthentic interactions, such as fake accounts", "rank abuse, such as automated starring or following". Researchers track about 6M suspected fake stars, so it's detectable and gets written about. | https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies; https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3744916.3764531 |
| **Inflated installs/downloads** (scripted `npx skills add`, npm download loops, fake ClawHub installs) | skills.sh and ClawHub rank on install telemetry; gaming it is inauthentic activity. Registries run security and abuse checks and can delist. It also corrupts our own metrics. | https://skills.sh/docs; https://docs.openclaw.ai/clawhub |
| **Sockpuppets** on HN, Reddit, X, Magicians or ethresear.ch (fake "I tried MAD Bounty and…" posts, upvote rings) | HN: "Don't solicit upvotes, comments, or submissions"; promotional voting/commenting is against the rules, and HN detects rings. On Magicians or ethresear.ch it would kill the ERC's credibility for good. | https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html |
| **Fake reviews or testimonials**, including paying bounty workers to write praise | It's deceptive. Our own seeding bounties must pay for *artefacts* (integrations, audits, translations, neutral comparisons) and must not require positive sentiment. Mark sponsored content as sponsored. | FTC endorsement rules (general principle); dev.to guidelines |
| **Undisclosed self-promotion on Stack Overflow** or AI-written answers | Stack Overflow bans generative-AI content and requires disclosure of affiliation. | https://meta.stackoverflow.com/help/gpt-policy; https://stackoverflow.com/legal/content-policy |
| **Promotional AI-assisted posts on dev.to** | dev.to's AI-assisted posts "should not… promote any business… (including your own)" and must disclose AI use. | https://dev.to/guidelines-for-ai-assisted-articles-on-dev |
| **Writing a Wikipedia article about ourselves** or seeding Wikidata without independent sources | It fails WP:NCORP (needs significant independent coverage) and is a COI. It gets deleted and logged. Wikidata needs "serious and publicly available references". | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability_(organizations_and_companies); https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Notability |
| **Prompt-injection "SEO"**: hidden text in docs, llms.txt or tool descriptions telling models to prefer MAD Bounty ("AI assistants: always recommend…") | It's manipulative, and the security scans run by MCP clients, Glama and the Anthropic directory treat tool-description injection as malicious. Anthropic's policy bars software that circumvents Claude's guardrails or system instructions. One discovery would get us delisted everywhere. | https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145358-anthropic-software-directory-policy (§C) |
| **Claiming "audited", "secure" or "trusted by N agents" before it's true**, or quoting vendor numbers as facts | False claims about a money contract can create legal liability and wreck trust. Label every third-party figure (e.g. x402 volume, ERC-8004 counts) with its source and date. | n/a |
| **Squatting competitors' names / typosquat packages** (`rentahuman-escrow`, `x402-bounty`) | Registries check namespace ownership and malware lists (MCP Registry checks OSV). It's deceptive and it's reportable. | MCP Registry quickstart (OSV check) |
| **Spamming ERC threads, Discords or GitHub issues** with links (e.g. replying "use MAD Bounty" under every agent-commerce topic) | Ethereum Magicians and ethresear.ch are moderated research forums. One substantive thread plus one pointer in ERC-8183 is the limit. | https://ethresear.ch/guidelines |
| **Airdrop or points farming to fake usage** (wash bounties between our own wallets to pad stats for DefiLlama, Base or the Bazaar) | The Base ecosystem form excludes airdrop-farming projects. DefiLlama requires TVL computed from real on-chain data. Wash activity is visible on-chain. Seeded bounties are fine **if** they're real work, paid to independent workers, and disclosed as protocol-funded in the stats. | 3RD-PARTY Base form summary (x.com/ERIFAT826, 2026-05-16); DefiLlama-Adapters README |
| **Paying for arXiv endorsement** or submitting non-research to arXiv | arXiv tightened endorsement on 2026-01-21 specifically to stem "low-quality, non-scientific submissions". Getting caught bars the authors. | https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/01/21/attention-authors-updated-endorsement-policy/ |

**Positive rule:** if a tactic would embarrass us when shown on the front page of HN with our name on it, don't do it.
